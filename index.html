How to play
  1. Drive with the arrow keys. Roll over a crate and it couples on as your last car.
  2. Your cars spell a word, front to back. Pull into the station when the train spells exactly its word.
  3. Wrong order? Space leaves the last car where it stands. The hooks show how many uncouples each line gives you.
  4. Later lines add a second station, turntables and sealed cars. Each one comes with a tip the first time you meet it.
